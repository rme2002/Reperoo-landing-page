Most people open an app like Reperoo when their phone is full. That's a few times a year, not a few times a day. A monthly subscription for that is a bad deal, and reviews of apps that switched to one say so loudly.

## Why not ads?

We looked at an ad-supported version and decided against it, for three reasons:

- **The ad moment is the trust moment.** The screen you'd see an ad on is the one where your photos are being verified and deleted. That's the worst possible place for a distraction.
- **Ads need the network.** Reperoo doesn't: anything stored on your iPhone copies with no connection at all, on a plane or up a mountain.
- **Ads need tracking.** Reperoo collects nothing, and we want to keep it that way.

## So: one purchase, €3.50

Reperoo costs **€3.50, once**. That unlocks everything on your Apple ID, for good. No renewals, no tiers, no ads.
