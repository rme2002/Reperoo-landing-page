## The Reperoo app

Reperoo collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no Reperoo server: your photos go from your iPhone to the drive you plug in, and nowhere else.

If Optimise iPhone Storage is on, iOS downloads full-size originals from your own iCloud before they are copied. That transfer is between your iPhone and Apple.

## This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. If you pause the animation or close the announcement bar, your browser remembers that choice on your device; it is never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store the email address you give us, plus your name and message if you add them. We use them only to email you when Reperoo launches and to answer your message. They are held by the service that runs our waitlist form, on our behalf, and we don't share them with anyone else. Every email we send has a way to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to remove your details at any time by replying to it.
